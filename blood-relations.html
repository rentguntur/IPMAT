Blood Relations

Understand it

The concept, explained properly

Relationship chains (“my grandfather's only son's daughter”) stack multiple hops, and each hop is easy to lose mentally. A quick family tree externalizes that stacking — you read the answer off a drawing instead of relying on working memory.

The fast way

Trick

Draw symbols as you read: + male, − female, = married, | parent-child.

Confusion

Gender-neutral terms like “cousin” or “sibling” get wrongly assumed a gender, causing contradictions later.

Fix

Mark gender-unknown people with a “?” until a clue explicitly confirms it.

Worked examples

Worked example — Easy

“She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son.” How is she related to Ravi (male)?

Grandfather's only son = Ravi's father. Her being his daughter makes her Ravi's sister.

Now you try — answers hidden

Pointing to a man, Priya said, “His mother is the only daughter of my mother.” How is the man related to Priya?
Priya's mother's only daughter = Priya herself. So the man's mother is Priya — the man is Priya's son.
