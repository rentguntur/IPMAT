Algebra & Quadratic Equations

Understand it

The concept, explained properly

If ax²+bx+c=0 has roots p and q, it factors as a(x−p)(x−q)=0. Expanding gives ax² − a(p+q)x + apq = 0. Matching coefficients with the original: p+q = −b/a and pq = c/a. These fall directly out of expanding the factored form — not arbitrary rules.

The fast way

Trick

Try factoring first (5 seconds) before the quadratic formula. For “which root pair” MCQs, test sum/product against the options directly.

Confusion

Sign errors when factoring — forgetting (x−a)(x−b) gives sum = −(a+b), not +(a+b).

Fix

Only fall back to the quadratic formula if factoring fails on two tries.

Worked examples

Worked example — Easy

Roots of x²−7x+k=0 are in ratio 2:5. Find k.

Roots = 2t, 5t. Sum = 7t = 7 → t=1. Roots = 2, 5. k = product = 10.

Now you try — answers hidden

If the sum of the roots of x²−(k+6)x+2k=0 is equal to twice the product of the roots, find k.
Sum = k+6, Product = 2k. Given: k+6 = 2(2k) → k+6=4k → 3k=6 → k=2.
Factorize and solve: x²−5x+6=0.
(x−2)(x−3)=0 → x=2 or x=3.
