Geometry & Mensuration

Understand it

The concept, explained properly

Area is built from two lengths multiplied together, so if every length scales by k, area scales by k². Volume is built from three lengths, so it scales by k³. This is a general geometric fact — it's why doubling a sphere's radius multiplies its volume by 8, not 2.

The fast way

Trick

Key formulas: Triangle=½base×height, Circle area=πr², Cylinder volume=πr²h, Cone volume=⅓πr²h (slant l=√(r²+h²)), Sphere volume=⁴⁄₃πr³.

Confusion

Cone surface area uses slant height; cone volume uses vertical height — easy to mix up.

Fix

Sketch the shape and label h (vertical) vs l (slant) before plugging into any formula.

Worked examples

Worked example — Easy

A sphere's radius is doubled. By what factor does volume increase?

Volume ∝ r³. Doubling r → 8× the volume.

The side of a cube is tripled. By what factor does its surface area increase?
Surface area ∝ side². Tripling side → 9×.
