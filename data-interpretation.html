Data Interpretation

Understand it

The concept, explained properly

The actual arithmetic in most DI questions is simple — the real bottleneck is finding the right numbers fast. Reading the question BEFORE looking at the data means you scan with a specific target instead of absorbing an entire dataset you won't fully use.

The fast way

Trick

Round to the nearest 5 or 10 unless answer options are very close together. Remember pie charts give %/degrees, not raw values — convert back using the stated total.

Confusion

Double-axis graphs (two different y-scales) are frequently misread as sharing one scale.

Fix

Set a hard time cap per DI set (5–6 minutes); if it isn't yielding answers, mark it and move on.

Worked examples

Worked example — Easy

A pie chart shows Marketing = 25% of a ₹12,00,000 budget. Marketing's budget?

25% = 1/4. 12,00,000/4 = ₹3,00,000.

Now you try — answers hidden

A bar chart shows sales of 200, 250, 300, 350 units over 4 months. Find the average monthly sales.
Sum=1100, average=1100/4=275 units.
A pie chart shows Region A=40%, Region B=35%, Region C=25% of total sales of ₹20,00,000. Find Region B's sales.
35% of 20,00,000 = ₹7,00,000.
